VAR check — before you hand in any writing

  • Capital letters and full stops in every sentence
  • Paragraphs — new idea, new paragraph
  • Speech punctuated correctly, new line for a new speaker
  • At least three ambitious words
  • Commas, apostrophes and other punctuation checked
  • Read it aloud once — does it sound right?
